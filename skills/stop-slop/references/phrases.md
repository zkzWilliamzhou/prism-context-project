# Phrases to cut (project-original condensation)

## Throat-clearing openers

- "In today's fast-paced world"
- "It is important to note that"
- "Here's what you need to know"
- "Let's dive in"
- "At the end of the day"

## Emphasis crutches

- "very", "really", "truly", "actually", "simply", "just"
- "powerful", "seamless", "robust", "game-changing"
- "in order to" → "to"
- "due to the fact that" → "because"

## Softening and hand-holding

- "It might be worth considering"
- "One possible approach could be"
- "Hopefully this helps"
- "As an AI language model"

## Adverbs that add no information

- "clearly", "obviously", "arguably", "fundamentally", "essentially"

Replace each with a fact, a number, or delete it.
