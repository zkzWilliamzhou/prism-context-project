# Structures to break (project-original condensation)

## Binary contrast

Bad: "It's not X, it's Y."
Better: state Y directly.

## Negative listing

Bad: "It isn't fast, it isn't simple, and it isn't free."
Better: name what it is.

## Dramatic fragmentation

Bad: "One line. One idea. One change."
Better: vary sentence length and put the point in a sentence.

## Rhetorical setup

Bad: "Why does this matter? Because..."
Better: answer without the question.

## False agency

Bad: "The complaint becomes a fix."
Better: name the person who fixes it.

## Tricolon

Bad: three parallel nouns or clauses in every paragraph.
Better: two items, or four uneven ones.

## Pull-quote ending

Bad: a paragraph that ends with a polished, quotable one-liner.
Better: end on the specific fact.

## Em dash

Remove it. Use a period, a comma, or a colon.
