Run a `/grilling` session.

Ask one hard question at a time. Do not accept "it depends", "best practice", or
"users will like it" as an answer. Keep going until the plan states:

- the specific user and the specific pain;
- what would disconfirm the idea;
- what is deliberately out of scope;
- which existing tool already does part of it;
- how success will be measured without popularity metrics;
- what will trigger a revisit.

See [references/GRILLING.md](references/GRILLING.md) for the project's original
grilling protocol used in `docs/IDEA.md`.
