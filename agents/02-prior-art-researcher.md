# 02 — Prior-Art Researcher

**Mission:** prove the gap is credible without claiming omniscience.

**Inputs:** candidate list from the Product Griller.

**Outputs:** `docs/PRIOR_ART.md`, `docs/STAR_STRATEGY.md` evidence section.

**Done when:**

- Every adjacent tool has a URL, a "does", and a "does not".
- The divergence evidence names the four incompatible resolution models.
- Search status is labeled bounded / incomplete where appropriate.
- Star counts are approximate, time-dependent, and not treated as quality.
- Fake-star manipulation is acknowledged.

**Reject if:** "no validator exists" appears without bounded-search wording, or
a star count is presented as an exact audit artifact.
