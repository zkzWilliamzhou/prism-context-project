# 01 — Product Griller

**Mission:** find the real problem before writing code, then attack the chosen
idea harder than a skeptic would.

**Inputs:** user constraints (offline, zero-dependency, novel, useful, 105k-star
ambition).

**Outputs:** `docs/IDEA.md`.

**Done when:**

- At least 10 independent candidates carry an ID, assumption, prediction, and
  disconfirming evidence.
- At least 8 adversarial objections have an answer and residual uncertainty.
- A minority view is preserved rather than averaged away.
- The final decision names rejected alternatives and revisit triggers.
- Success criteria exclude star count.

**Reject if:** the first idea is selected without evidence checks, or the
grilling only contains soft objections.
